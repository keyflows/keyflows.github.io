About KeyFlows

KeyFlows is an account-free typing practice website designed to help people build typing speed, accuracy, consistency, and confidence at their own pace.

What you can practice

  • Timed and word-count typing tests, including custom text.
  • Keyboard lessons and guided exercises.
  • Typing games with story-based passages.
  • Reading and typing exercises, daily challenges, and practice recommendations.
  • Local statistics, progress indicators, achievements, and data export.

How it works

Core practice runs in your browser. No KeyFlows account is required. Settings and practice summaries are stored locally when browser storage is available. The static edition does not provide public leaderboards, multiplayer, or cross-device sync.

Who makes KeyFlows?

KeyFlows is developed by Ayush Nandanwar. Visit GitHub or LinkedIn.

Certificates

Certificates are informal personal achievement downloads generated on your device. They are not accredited or independently verified qualifications.
