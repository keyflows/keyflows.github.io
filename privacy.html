Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

KeyFlows is an account-free typing practice website. This policy describes information handled by the website and your choices.

Information stored in your browser

Practice settings and progress information may be stored locally in your browser, including preferences, session summaries, streaks, achievements, challenge results, and custom text you choose to save. KeyFlows does not operate an account system or a backend that receives practice sessions.

KeyFlows is designed not to retain a stream of your keystrokes or typed passages in session history. Custom text you save is kept in browser storage. A full backup may include saved custom text; CSV session exports are intended to contain session summaries.

Exporting and deleting data

Use Settings to export a spreadsheet or full backup, restore a backup, or reset local data. You can also clear site data through your browser settings. Clearing browser data may permanently remove information that you have not exported.

Advertising and third-party services

KeyFlows uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google and advertising partners may use cookies, identifiers, device/browser information, and information about ad or site interactions to deliver, measure, and personalize advertising, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements, and their policies. These services are operated by third parties; KeyFlows does not control their processing.

Review Google advertising technologies and Google My Ad Center for information and controls.

Cookies and browser technologies

Core features use browser storage. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies. Browser controls can clear or restrict these technologies, though doing so may affect saved practice data or ad preferences.

Children

KeyFlows is a general-purpose typing practice website and is not specifically directed at children. Please contact the site operator if you believe a child has shared personal information through an external communication channel.

Security and sensible use

Browser-stored information is subject to the security of your device and browser profile. Do not use custom text or browser storage for passwords, financial information, or sensitive personal data. KeyFlows cannot guarantee uninterrupted service or prevent browser data loss.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the product evolves. For privacy questions, use the developer's contact routes on the Contact page. Avoid sharing sensitive information in public issue trackers.
