Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

KeyFlows is an account-free typing practice website maintained by Ayush Nandanwar. This policy describes information handled by KeyFlows, its hosting provider, and advertising services, and your choices.

Information stored in your browser

Practice settings and progress information may be stored locally in your browser, including preferences, session summaries, streaks, achievements, challenge results, and custom text you choose to save. KeyFlows does not operate an account system or a backend that receives practice sessions.

KeyFlows is designed not to retain a stream of your keystrokes or typed passages in session history. Custom text you save is kept in browser storage. A full backup may include saved custom text; CSV session exports are intended to contain session summaries.

Hosting and technical information

KeyFlows is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Your browser also connects to third-party services when their resources are used. These providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, request details, and timestamps under their own privacy policies. See the GitHub Pages information and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Exporting and deleting data

Use Settings to export a spreadsheet or full backup, restore a backup, or reset local data. You can also clear site data through your browser settings. Clearing browser data may permanently remove information that you have not exported.

Advertising and third-party services

KeyFlows integrates Google AdSense. When advertisements are enabled and displayed, Google and third-party advertising vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, browser or device information, and information about interactions with advertisements to deliver, measure, and personalize advertising.

Advertising may be personalized based on your visits to KeyFlows and other websites, subject to applicable privacy settings, consent requirements, and advertising policies. Third-party vendors and advertising networks may also use cookies or similar technologies to serve advertisements.

You can manage Google's advertising personalization settings through Google Ads Settings. You may also review available third-party advertising opt-out options at AboutAds.info.

For additional information, review Google's advertising technologies and Google's Privacy Policy. KeyFlows does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party advertising providers.

Cookies and browser technologies

Core features use browser storage. Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies. Browser controls can clear or restrict these technologies, though doing so may affect saved practice data or ad preferences.

Children

KeyFlows is a general-purpose typing practice website and is not specifically directed at children. Please contact the site operator if you believe a child has shared personal information through an external communication channel. Advertising settings and practices must comply with applicable requirements relating to children and child-directed content.

Security and sensible use

Browser-stored information is subject to the security of your device and browser profile. Do not use custom text or browser storage for passwords, financial information, or sensitive personal data. KeyFlows cannot guarantee uninterrupted service or prevent browser data loss.

Changes and contact

This policy may change as the product evolves. For privacy questions or requests, use the developer's contact routes on the Contact page. Avoid sharing sensitive information in public issue trackers.
