CLEAR AND CONSISTENT

How scoring works

Raw WPMTotal characters typed ÷ 5 ÷ elapsed minutes.

Net WPMRaw WPM minus incorrect characters ÷ elapsed minutes ÷ 5. Mistakes remain in the penalty even after correction.

AccuracyCorrect keystrokes ÷ all attempted keystrokes × 100. Backspaces don’t erase an earlier mistake from this measure.

Skipped charactersAre not counted as attempts. A word counts as completed when its trailing space is entered (or at the end of the prompt).

Speed is hidden briefly at the start to avoid noisy readings. WPM is shown only after at least 3 seconds for a more reliable reading. Results use actual elapsed time and only completed sessions are saved.
